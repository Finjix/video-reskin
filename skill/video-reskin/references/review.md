# Review

Gemini compares each complete generated segment with the source at 6 frames/s on the same clock. No independent seam or transition review is performed.

Overall segment review and main-agent verification judge the impression at normal playback speed. Allow a small number of subtle defects, brief minor clipping and small shape or texture inconsistencies that ordinary viewers are unlikely to notice in motion. Paused frames, magnification and close frame-by-frame inspection are not the acceptance standard. Report only defects readily noticeable during normal playback or materially changing the main content or action. Tolerated imperfections are not unresolved issues, must not fail a segment or trigger retries, and require pass with an empty issues list when nothing else is wrong.

UI, HUD and text use a relaxed standard in both Gemini review and main-agent verification. Mild blur, ghosting or jelly-like deformation during changes or movement is acceptable when they look normal when static and their overall placement remains reasonably stable. Do not require pixel-perfect shapes or frame-by-frame text and number matching during changes. Report only obvious drifting, severe distortion, persistent abnormal appearance when static, or major missing or added elements. Tolerated imperfections must not fail a segment or trigger a quality retry.

Reference-image-only rule: generated reference images must preserve every source logo and all text unchanged in content, lettering, font, color, shape, size, placement and original appearance, including numbers, brand marks, warnings and HUD labels. Reject generated restyling, realistic conversion, embossing, relighting, distortion, substitution, addition or removal even if realistic coverage is complete. Compare against the source image and retain blur, occlusion and overlap already present in it; do not reposition elements or reconstruct hidden lettering. For each failed reference image, open the unchanged original source and generated reference files listed under images in the report; do not create a comparison sheet. Replace noncompliant reference images before rendering. This strict rule does not change video-generation prompts, tail continuity checks or the relaxed segment-review rules above.

Gameplay joins use a fixed 4-frame transition (1/6 second at 24 fps), spanning the outgoing last two frames and incoming first two frames. Outgoing weights are 3/4, 1/2, 1/2, 1/4; incoming weights are 1/4, 1/2, 1/2, 3/4. For the outgoing frames, overlay the frozen incoming first frame; for the incoming frames, overlay the frozen outgoing last frame. The incoming third frame is fully incoming. Boundaries into or out of original/non-game footage and end cards are hard cuts. Preserve total frame count and audio alignment. There is no seam or post-transition review.

For every segment or reference-image issue, the main agent must open the independent native-size source/result frames or original reference images listed in the report, verify the reported problem, and scan each entire picture for other defects. For segment issues, use the independent frames to locate and understand the problem, then judge its impact in motion; replay the corresponding comparison clip when stills alone cannot establish visibility during normal playback. Only defects exceeding the applicable acceptance threshold require correction. False positives, including tolerated minor defects incorrectly reported as failures, require a fresh model review under this standard before normal approval. A normal approval requires a passed current segment check.

After the initial generation, allow at most two quality retries (three versions total). If none passes, compare all three, inspect reported evidence, and use select-best with the selected generation ID, comparison findings and remaining defects. Keep failed verdicts and label acceptance best_available; continue subsequent segments and assembly. Service failures are recorded separately, not additional quality retries.

Tail suitability compares the previous generated last frame with the next source first frame ONLY for same-shot continuity, never material realism or rendering quality. It is not a video quality verdict. An unsuitable tail selects a converted first frame for the next segment; it cannot invalidate an approved segment or trigger/count a video quality retry. Both suitable and unsuitable decisions are saved. No main-agent image review is required for this reference-selection decision.

Media inputs: Gemini survey uses the full-size, silent 24 fps working copy (H.264 CRF 16, slow, yuv420p). Full-segment comparison videos use CRF 16, slow, yuv420p, retaining each panel at working-copy dimensions, 24 fps with 6 fps Gemini sampling and the inline-size bitrate cap. Gemini reference-image review, tail continuity and reference selection send the original image files without resizing or JPEG conversion, including candidate references. Main-agent issue review opens independent native-size frames and original reference files; no reduced or stitched comparison images. Gemini reports issue times/descriptions; local FFmpeg extracts the frames.
